Privacy policy

Last updated May 1, 2026

SNED-LINK+ collects only the information needed to connect you with your child's teacher-verified records: your account details, and the behavior, IEP and progress data your child's teacher publishes to this portal.

Your child's data is never shared outside the school's care team. AI-generated insights are reviewed by a teacher before you can see them, and are used only to support recommendations — never to make decisions automatically.

You may request a copy of your data, or ask that it be corrected, by contacting your child's teacher or the school administrator at any time.

Terms of service

Using the Parent Portal

This account is provided to you by your child's school for the sole purpose of monitoring their progress. Access is by invitation only and may be revoked by the school at any time.

The Parent Portal is read-only. All behavior updates, insights and documents originate from the Teacher Portal and are shown here after a teacher reviews them — you cannot edit records directly.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for notifying the school if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

Help & support

Frequently asked
Why can't I edit Joshua's behavior records?
The Parent Portal is read-only by design, so every record you see has already been reviewed and approved by a teacher.
How do I get a missing IEP document?
Open the Vault and tap "Request a document" to send a request straight to your child's teacher — including the IEP and Behavioral Monitoring Kit for SNED Learners.
What does the Teacher Verified mark mean?
It confirms a teacher reviewed the AI-assisted data before it was published to your dashboard.
Who can see my child's information?
Only you and the school's care team — teachers and authorized staff assigned to your child.
