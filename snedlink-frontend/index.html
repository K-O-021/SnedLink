ABOUT THE SYSTEM

About SNED-LINK+

SNED-LINK+ is a web-based Special Needs Education Management System designed to improve collaboration among administrators, teachers, and parents through secure monitoring, AI-assisted recommendations, and centralized Individualized Education Program (IEP) management.

Vision

To provide a secure, intelligent, and collaborative digital platform that supports the holistic development of learners with special educational needs.
